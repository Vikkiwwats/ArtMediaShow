# Equipment photo expansion — 2026-10-17

The owner supplied all 90 inventory rows. The expanded catalogue has 33 featured cards and images for 74 inventory positions: 48 catalogue/model-family photographs and 26 explicitly labelled generic illustrations. No model names or quantities were inferred from images.

## Photographs

Photographs are supplier/manufacturer catalogue references, not photographs of the company’s individual rental units. The catalogue labels model-family examples as “Фото серии”, copies/CUSTOM examples as “Фото оригинала”, and amplifier-only photographs as “На фото усилитель”. Model revisions which were not supplied have not been added to the inventory names.

Opaque product photographs were isolated from their backgrounds with the built-in imagegen background-extraction tool, retaining the supplied reference product and its photographed angle. Original source images remain the reference for product identity. Transparent source images were kept without generative changes. Web encoding and sizing are optimized for delivery. The original Outline Mantas 28, Ampeg SVT-7PRO and Atomic 3000 framing uses native SVG and embedded catalogue photographs.

## Generic illustrations

Generic fixture, LED panel, stage-structure and effects images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and are explicitly labelled “Визуализация” on the site. They do not assert a specific manufacturer, exact branded model, physical rental unit or event photograph. The transparent cutouts are composed on `assets/equipment/orange-stage.webp` with CSS.

The source inventory does not identify exact manufacturers for these lights, screens, smoke machines and structures. Shared type illustrations are reused for closely related catalogue entries; they are not presented as separate photographs of different inventory versions.

Prompt sets and final asset paths are retained in [IMAGE-PROMPTS.json](IMAGE-PROMPTS.json).
