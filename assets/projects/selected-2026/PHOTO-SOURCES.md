# Selected Art Media Show event photographs

16 supplied screenshots → 13 new event projects / 16 processed photographs; the 5 earlier gallery projects are retained.

Method: built-in image_gen edit, followed by WebP encoding with Sharp. Original uploads were not overwritten. Restoration crops Instagram UI, gently corrects light/colour and reduces compression artifacts; these are AI-assisted restorations of supplied event frames, not camera originals.

The photo viewer preserves full image aspect ratio. Card covers crop responsively. Each photo has a 720px thumbnail and a larger image for the viewer.
